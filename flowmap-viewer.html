See your world's water

This is a made-up sample island. Load a flow map from Straumr to see a world of yours.

  1. In game, open the console (F5), type devcommands, then straumr map.
  2. Find BepInEx/config/Straumr-flowmap.json in your mod profile.
  3. Drop it on this page, or choose it here.

Dedicated server: set [Diagnostics] WorldGenCommand = flowmap in wackah.straumr.cfg; the file lands in the server's BepInEx/config. The file stays in this browser and is never uploaded.

The map shows the world's shape and rivers (Riverheim's or Valheim's) and Straumr's water physics. Rivers and Lakes' streams aren't drawn.

A flow map exported in a world carries the water players built there too (Built water): its dams, kept pools and cofferdams as they stood, and a lab server's last damflood.

Seed generator: in Valheim (the main menu is fine), open the console (F5), type devcommands, then straumr atlas. It opens this page from your own game and builds any seed with your mods' settings.

Generate a world

Type a seed in the bar above and press Generate. Your game builds it with its own mods and settings, usually in 40 to 110 seconds (the rivers' levels and flood plains are some 20 of them), then shows the whole world.

Or press This world to map the world you're in, without generating anything.

Zoom in and the game fills in 2 m ground and 4 m currents, square by square.

Fords, Water power and Drift (R): where a river can be waded across at the tide on the clock, where the water carries the most power for a wheel, and where something floating goes.

Built water: in the world you're in (This world), its dams with their pools, walls, crests and ways round, its kept pools and cofferdams, and the river below each dam (its share of the flow, the surge), live from the game every 1.5 s. Click one for its numbers, a level to draw its pool at, and a time scrubber back through what the page has seen.

Water looks wrong somewhere? Press D for the debug view (which river owns each 4 m cell, its head, bank and line) and click a cell for its numbers.
